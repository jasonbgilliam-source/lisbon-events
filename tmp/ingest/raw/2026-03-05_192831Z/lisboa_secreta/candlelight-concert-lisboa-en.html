One of the most memorable plans for 2026 is located in the heart of Lisbon: classical music and thousands of candles in stunning locations.

Everything from legendary rock hits to the Best of the 80s is being (re)interpreted by talented musicians. And you can find all upcoming Candlelight concerts here!

2026 calls for gentle horizons: candlelit nights, strings that breathe slowly, a calm that tastes like discreet luxury. Between tributes that take on new life and special editions emerging throughout the year, the best Candlelight concerts in Lisbon suggest moments to slow down, take care of the spirit, and make new memories. From festive sparkle to serene glow, there is a gentle invitation: intimate music, time well spent, light that tunes us in.

Candlelight featured

Candlelight: The Best of Vivaldi

From the vibrant heat of summer to the piercing silence of winter, through the rebirth of spring and the colors of autumn, Vivaldi’s “The Four Seasons” paints nature in music with rare intensity. In four movements full of life, the work evokes landscapes, emotions, and memories without the need for words —it is timeless, recognizable from the first chords, and always capable of surprising us, as if we were hearing it for the first time.

Candlelight: The Lord of the Rings

Candlelight: The Lord of the Rings arrives in Lisbon to transform the epic music of Middle Earth into an intimate and captivating experience. By the light of thousands of candles, a string quartet performs some of the most iconic soundtracks from the saga, creating a magical atmosphere that transports the audience to Hobbiton, Rivendell, and beyond. It is a concert designed for fans and the curious, where the emotion of the music intersects with the beauty of the setting, in one of the most special ways to experience Lisbon differently.

Candlelight venues in Lisbon

The best Candlelight concerts in Lisbon take place in stunning and iconic venues, such as the Church of Santa Catarina, the Royal Treasury Museum, and the Altis Grand Hotel. These spaces provide an intimate and enchanting atmosphere, lit by thousands of candles, offering a unique multisensory experience.

Reasons to go to Candlelight concerts

There are several reasons to attend Candlelight concerts in Lisbon, where the delicate glow of thousands of candles transforms iconic venues into enchanting spaces that captivate all the senses. These concerts feature highly talented musicians whose passion brings every note to life, creating an intimate and magical atmosphere. They break down barriers around classical music, making it accessible and engaging for everyone. Each season, new tributes to classical music and popular artists offer fresh and memorable experiences for music lovers.
